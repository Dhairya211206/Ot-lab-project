(e) CONCLUSION
======================================================================
1. Logistic Regression ranked breakdown risk; QAOA picked ['B0', 'B3', 'B4', 'B9'] for the 4 slots, matching brute force.
2. The QUBO penalty enforced 'exactly 4'; the ML-driven choice prevents more expected breakdowns than mileage-only or random baselines.
3. Parallel evaluation of all 2^10 energies gave speed-up < p because of process start-up overhead; QAOA is a demo at 10 qubits, classical brute force is still faster here.

Limitations: synthetic data; QAOA p=2 gives a low probability for the optimum; small n hides parallel gains.
